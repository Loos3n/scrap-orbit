Scrap Orbit privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

In short: your station is saved on your own device. Nothing leaves it unless you sign in for cloud save or add friends, and you can delete that cloud copy from inside the game at any time. The game has no analytics and no tracking. In the Android app, ads only play when you tap an optional ad offer.

What stays on your device

Your progress (scrap, machines, crew, settings and so on) is saved in your browser's or phone's own storage. It never leaves the device unless you use cloud save. Clearing the site's data, uninstalling the app, or using Settings, Reset progress removes it.

Cloud save (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Google in Settings, the game uses Google Firebase to sign you in and keep a copy of your save. This stores:

  • your Google account's sign-in id, name, email address and profile picture link, which Firebase Authentication keeps so you can sign in again;
  • your save and when it was backed up, how much scrap you have salvaged in all, and how many times you have relocated.

Only you can read your save. It is used for nothing except bringing your station back on another device.

Friend codes (optional)

If you open Friends while signed in, the game publishes a small profile under your friend code: the station name you choose, a small picture of your station, and a few numbers from your save (such as scrap salvaged, relocations and achievements). Any signed-in player who knows your code can see it. Gifts that friends send you are kept until you open them.

Ads (Android app only)

The Android app can show an optional ad when you tap an ad offer (for example, to double your offline earnings). Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect device information such as the advertising id to show and measure ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Ad offers can be switched off in Settings. The web version shows no ads.

Reminders (Android app only)

If you allow notifications, reminders (storage full, research done, crew home) are scheduled on your phone itself. Nothing is sent to a server.

Deleting your data

  • Cloud save and friend code: open Settings, Cloud save, Delete my cloud save. This deletes your cloud save, your friend profile and any unopened gifts, deletes your sign-in account where Google allows it, and signs you out.
  • On your device: Settings, Reset progress, or uninstall the app or clear the site's data.

Children

Scrap Orbit is a general audience game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and every online feature is optional and off until you sign in.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone, except the Google services named above (Firebase for sign-in and cloud save, AdMob for optional ads in the Android app), which process it to provide those features.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions or deletion requests: use the developer contact email listed on the game's store page.
